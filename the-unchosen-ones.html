// THE_PREMISE · A_GRIMDARK_MILITARY-TEMPORAL_IDENTITY_HORROR //

A future-displaced artifact.
A colony that learned to survive by becoming someone else.

Set after the Monastery Schism but before The Sablean Conspiracy, this is the mission that hardens Maeryth Vonn's strained trust in Selena into lasting moral fracture. A future-displaced umbral artifact from the New Sablean timeline — the Veltryn Stone — has been hidden for generations inside a sealed colony built around an ancient temple-lab. Selena, using intelligence from Lord Vyktor, activates Maeryth and a specially prepared Sisters of Ira cell to extract samples before the colony perfects containment and escapes.

ARTIFACT
The Veltryn Stone — 4.6m umbral meteorite. Does not swap bodies. Substitutes the soul-memory-identity essence inside the same body with an alternate-timeline version of that person.
PHENOMENON
The Altnet — a valid alternate identity-state inhabiting the body that belonged to the original person. Not illusion, not clone. Some more violent, some more disciplined, some simply who they might have become.
CONTAINMENT
A temporal shadow bubble masks the colony's population and stabilizes affected persons — who suffer rapid soul-death if they leave its radius.
VYKTOR'S ROLE
Knows of the artifact for decades but never retrieves it — proximity risks his own replacement. Uses Selena's cult as the hand that reaches what he cannot safely touch.
SELENA'S TRUE GOAL
Not merely extraction. Controlled betrayal disguised as containment — proof that vesselhood is engineering, not divine mystery.

Selena's public objective is extraction. Her hidden objective: test Maeryth under identity pressure, observe Altnet-trained Sisters inside the artifact's field, destroy the colony before Vyktor can claim it, and erase evidence of decades of prior Sisters of Ira involvement.

// SISTER_MAERYTH_VONN · FALSE-IDENTITY_FIELD_COMMANDER //

The Mourning Face.
The Woman Who Wore Seven Griefs.

Maeryth Vonn is an infiltration officer, identity-theft specialist, and field commander — officially dead under three civil registers, elite within the Sisters but not Selena's confidante. Her signature technique is Pilgrim Skin: a temporary identity constructed from stolen memory, shadow residue, and grief imprint. The stronger the emotional material, the more convincing the disguise — and the more dangerous it becomes to her sense of self.

AGE
32. Born Veyr Hollow, a fog-covered Sablean border settlement.
TITLES
The Mourning Face · The Woman Who Wore Seven Griefs · False Daughter of the Northern Road
SIGNATURE
Pilgrim Skin — identity constructed from stolen memory, clothing, shadow residue, speech rhythm, grief imprint
IRA-STATE
The Mourning Face — eyes burn amber-gold, veil darkens into living shadow, stolen griefs become visible as faces beneath her aura
DEEPEST FEAR
Becoming a hollow vessel for other people's memories — a composite of every identity she has worn
CENTRAL Q.
"If every version of the self is possible, what makes one version responsible?" Her answer: choice under pressure, not purity or origin.
Taken into First Veil training at thirteen — survived the Mirror Trial by refusing to speak her original name even as a hallucination of her mother begged her to come home
First major success: the House Velshar Substitution — replaced a dead courier to redirect a royal audit from a Sisters relic route
Promoted to Infiltration Officer after wearing a murdered abbess's identity for nine days without breaking character
The Monastery Schism becomes the defining rupture of her life — a dead pilgrim's shadow whispers her childhood name, and its memories remain inside her afterward
Enters Veltryn Null already more morally alert than Selena wants her to be — loyal enough to accept the mission, not blind enough to accept Selena's explanations without suspicion

"Wear the grief, not the face." / "A clean lie leaves no echo." / "Count the shadow before the body." — Maeryth's field doctrine

// OPERATIONAL_DOCTRINE · SUSPICION_IS_SURVIVABLE · CONTRADICTION_IS_LETHAL //

Not "sneak in, kill guards."
Legible permissibility.

Veltryn Null is a layered verification environment where the Loom judges continuity and the Axiom Host enforces the verdict through architecture. Maeryth's team survives not by looking innocent — innocence is too clean — but by looking legibly permissible: tired travelers, grief-bearing petitioners, technical assistants, lost pilgrims. Every disguise Maeryth wears has a moral invoice. She is not putting on clothing. She is using someone's wound as a credential.

Maeryth's first instinct is never "Where are the enemies?" It is "What is this place trying to prove?" A lamp dims two shades when she passes — the Host noticed but did not reject her. A mirror holds a teammate's shadow a heartbeat too long — she is not being read as foreign, but as intermittently known. She turns all of it into route decisions. She is managing what kind of story the city is allowed to tell about them.

// THE_LOOM · THE_NULL_PROVENANCE_MARK · THE_AXIOM_HOST //

The Loom can hesitate.
The Host cannot.

The colony's judgment architecture in two parts. The Loom is a mobile ceremonial instrument operated by Sisters — a Green Scan stabilizes an unstable subject long enough to be read accurately; a Red Scan records a failure of lawful continuity. Before any mark is applied, the operating Sister must pass through the Seven Withholdings: Error, Trauma, Possession, Displacement, Incomplete Data, Mercy, Witness. All seven must fail before judgment is permitted.

The Null Provenance Mark is not physical — it's written into the relationship between a person's body, shadow, reflection, name, memory, and spatial permission. It cannot be removed, transferred, or disguised, because it follows the underlying contradiction, not the surface. The Axiom Host is the distributed enforcement network embedded throughout the colony — gates, mirrors, lamps, floors. It does not detect trespass. It detects unbelonging. Confirmed match → Severance Protocol. Instant. No warning. No negotiation.

DOCTRINE
Mercy lives upstream, at the Loom, because it's operated by a person. Nothing downstream, at the Host, is allowed to second-guess a verdict already written.
MAERYTH'S CASE
Avayne Null runs the extended Seven Withholdings on Maeryth across the entire mission — and never completes it. Maeryth's conditioned identity stack doesn't match any classification the Loom was built to handle. Her hesitation isn't mercy. It's diagnostic failure.

// PROVENANCE_HEAT · BELONGING_PRESSURE · THE_RISK_ECONOMY //

A permanent one-star state.
Suspicion is survivable. Contradiction is lethal.

The moment Maeryth's team crosses into Veltryn Null, the Axiom Mesh is already sampling them — a permanent Provisional Observation baseline. Three meters interact constantly: Watchload (active attention from civilians, clerks, patrols, nodes), Continuity Stress (how unstable a disguise or borrowed grief has become), and Red Jurisdiction (how capable the current area is of enforcing a verdict).

1★
Provisional Observation
Default state. Green lamps turn toward the team; mirrors hold half a breath too long; clerks repeat names. Survivable, active almost always.
2★
Active Interest
The Axiom Mesh compares route behavior. Lamps brighten when the team passes. Low-level Ytixian assistants begin appearing in visited locations.
3★
Audit Pressure
The city tests them through social mazes, name checks, reflection-delay traps, Green Quiet. The player must actively reduce pressure.
4★
Red Proximity
Correction Zones open ahead. Route options narrow. Directorate patrols coordinate with Ytixian observers. A formal case may begin.
5★
Marked / Near-Marked
Sudden-death nightmare state. Safehouses degrade. Any Host-capable node can become lethal. This is why the Interceptor can no longer enter.
Every Solution Creates Residue

Grief borrowing gains social permission but harms the donor. Hacking a node gets a route but gives Kethrel something to trace. Violence removes an obstacle but changes the Host's legal map.

Provenance Assembly

Not "find three keys" — objectives require social, technical, and continuity permission simultaneously, each obtainable multiple ways, none of them clean.

"Marked for Death" Mode

Maeryth enters already marked. Every Host-capable threshold is a possible execution site. This is what the Interceptor's life feels like, made playable.

// CANON_DIGEST · THE_DETONATION_AND_THE_FINAL_HORROR //

Not that the mission failed.
That Selena can call it success.

// FINAL_VOLUME_STATEMENT //

A Containment Massacre,
Not a Clean Victory

The sleeper Sister detonates the artifact after samples are secured. The temporal shadow bubble collapses. Affected identities destabilize. Some Altnets die instantly; others suffer accelerated soul-death; children and civilians may be lost. The main artifact is destroyed. Vyktor is denied the prize. Selena gains the samples. This prevents a greater catastrophe while creating a moral wound that cannot be justified away.

Maeryth returns with surviving samples and incomplete survivors. Selena receives the outcome as a success. Maeryth does not. She learns Selena had been cultivating Altnet children as Sisters for years, that prior Sisters were sacrificed across decades, and that her own team was partly a test population.

Maeryth falsifies part of her final report. The strongest hidden lie: that partial stabilization comes from self-chosen identity anchors, not from Selena's conditioning. Selena wants evidence that identity can be engineered from above. Maeryth discovers it can be protected from within — and keeps that one truth for herself.

// CENTRAL_THEMES //
Identity Is Choice Under Pressure

Not origin. A body can survive while the self inside it is replaced entirely.

A Cult Can Save Children and Still Weaponize Them

The Altnet Sisters were rescued — and then turned into exactly what rescued them.

Containment Can Be Necessary and Still Be Atrocity

Both things are true about the detonation at once. Neither cancels the other.

Some Betrayals Are Monstrous Because They Are Strategically Correct

Ysra may be right about containment. She is still committing mass murder under cover of Maeryth's mission.

Maeryth's greatest danger is not being replaced by another version of herself. It is becoming the version Selena always wanted her to be.
