// FACTION_IDENTITY · THE_PHILOSOPHICAL_PROBLEM //

Not apostates.
Something more theologically specific.

Selena's doctrine is faith managed by authority — it does not have evidence, it has hierarchy; not verification, but obedience. The Veltryn Stone offered the converted Sisters something Ira's promise never had: proof. When they felt the artifact's field reorganize identity at a level they had trained their whole lives to understand, they did not experience betrayal. They experienced completion.

Their argument against Maeryth specifically: "You steal identities for a woman who lies to you. You have never verified whether the framework you serve is real or whether you simply lack a better option. You are the most gifted identity specialist in Selena's operation, and you have never once verified your own." This is the argument they use on Mirevane. It will almost work.

// THE_SEVEN_SISTERS_OF_YTIXUS · INDIVIDUAL_CANON //

Seven judges.
Seven functions of one verdict.

I
Avayne Null
The Parallax Eye / The Red Sister
RED_JUDGE

Senior judge of the Loom — the only Sister authorized to complete a full red classification without council. Red gems at her forehead are passive observation nodes reading shadow-weight and identity coherence from anyone nearby. Not swapped suddenly — changed slowly across months of observation until she concluded the substituted version of herself was epistemologically superior, and made no effort to resist it. That was a decision, not passivity.

Identifies Maeryth's cell within forty minutes of the echo verge crossing — she trained under the same Mask-Class doctrine lineage. Runs the Seven Withholdings on Maeryth and never completes them, because Maeryth's conditioned identity stack matches no classification the Loom was built to handle. Her hesitation isn't mercy. It's diagnostic failure.

SYMBOL: Observation without mercy — the judgment that contains both options and chooses finally
II
Serath Moiv
The Green Path / The Stabilizer
GREEN_JUDGE

Operates the Loom's stabilization function through gauntlets housing the green-scan mechanism. The only convert who volunteered through what Ytixian record calls a voluntary Green Exposure — she asked to be stabilized under green light to understand what the Loom would see in her, and survived with her identity mostly intact.

First point of contact for potential converts, approaching through intellectual warmth rather than doctrinal pressure. Engaged Mirevane Korr first, recognizing her as a records-keeper who would respond to systematic rather than emotional appeal. Never says the Sisters of Ira are wrong — only asks questions Mirevane cannot answer. This is the most dangerous kind of seduction.

SYMBOL: Gentleness weaponized — warmth as the delivery of a fatal idea
III
Dreya Orvoss
The Spiral Depth / The Colony Thread
INFILTRATION

Continuity auditor for social infiltration inside the colony's civilian life. Her substitution was so gradual she cannot identify the exact moment the original Dreya ended. She considers this philosophically important: continuity never broke — it bent. Substitution is not death. It is evolution, and refusing to accept it is the only fatal response.

First to appear near Maeryth's team in a civilian role, running a market contact when Thaneira passes. Thaneira feels recognition she cannot account for — not of Dreya's face, but her movement pattern. The observation chain begins here. This plants the first seed of the team's paranoia.

SYMBOL: Continuity as adaptation — the most comfortable lie about what the self is
IV
Valyss Sol
The Key Bearer / The First Verdict
FOUNDER

Leader of the first Sisters of Ira cell Selena ever sent — fifteen years ago. Fully and violently swapped within three months near the inner chamber; the original Valyss died the same moment her replacement appeared. Immediately recognized the artifact as something Selena had profoundly misunderstood, and built the Sisters of Ytixus from the converted membership of every subsequent entry cell.

Has documented fifteen years of Selena's operational evidence and holds a Null Provenance Mark pre-authorized in absentia for Selena — a senior-enough judge can complete the full Seven Withholdings against someone not physically present. The verdict is already written. It simply has no Host node near Selena yet to read it. She isn't planning an assassination. She's carrying a sentence that's already final and waiting for proximity.

SYMBOL: Brilliance in service of a verdict that cannot account for what it destroys
V
Kethrel Onn
The Cloak Host / The Node Keeper
INFRASTRUCTURE

Manages the distributed Axiom Host node network throughout the colony rather than the judgment instrument itself. Reveals — factually, without triumph — that the Interceptor and Watcher have already been marked by the very security architecture built by people Selena sent. The Host does not ask whether a marked subject was once a Sister of Ira. It only asks whether continuity survives observation.

SYMBOL: Ideology encoded into infrastructure — the horror of automatic enforcement
VI
Naeriva Thael
The Raven Circuit / The Quiet Watcher
PSYCHOLOGICAL

Primary psychological engagement specialist — spent the most time with Mirevane Korr. Red gems at her wrists are reflection-delay readers measuring the exact gap between a subject's action and their shadow's response. Her method is not doctrine but genuine scholarly curiosity about Mirevane's documentation, which is what makes her so dangerous — the engagement isn't performance.

The trigger: she tells Mirevane she does not recommend the Loom's green scan, because Mirevane's recent false-memory exposure makes her too unstable for clean observation. Telling a records-keeper her data is too dirty to verify is the precise move. Mirevane asks for the scan herself.

SYMBOL: Intellectual integrity weaponized — truth-seeking turned into a trigger
VII
Yrsath Korr
The Marked Blade / The Verdict's Edge
MANUAL_OVERRIDE

The faction's only close-combat operative — the manual override for targets the Host can't reach. Carries her verdict literally in her hand rather than letting architecture deliver it. Her transition to violence took years, following one consistent logic: if observation reveals an unrecoverable contradiction and the Host is unavailable, the verdict is still valid and requires enforcement. She kills calmly, with ceremony, without hatred — which is more disturbing than rage.

The Sister Maeryth must personally kill. Maeryth wins not through superior technique, but because Yrsath's system requires the self to be predictable — and Maeryth, at her most defended, acts from herself rather than any tactical model. You cannot predict what a self does when it acts from its actual core.

SYMBOL: The end-state of judgment without mercy — honest embodiment of an insufficient philosophy is more dangerous than hypocrisy

// THE_INTERCEPTOR_&_THE_WATCHER · THE_LAST_TWO_ORIGINAL_SISTERS //

Not Sisters of Ytixus.
What survives being marked and left outside.

The last two original Sisters of Ira, running the final degraded remnant of Selena's external perimeter operation for years — marked by the Loom, unable to re-enter, surviving in a mission that has slowly become something else entirely.

The Interceptor
Sister Verath Nol

Maintains the communications relay at the colony's outskirts — mission-command posture preserved for years after most of who she relayed to were converted, killed, or transformed. Has assembled a document proving Selena orchestrated an atrocity, but never transmitted it.

She is preserved by her mission like a specimen in amber. The open question: is remaining unchanged across years here a sign of identity strength, or a sign the environment found nothing in her worth changing?

The Watcher
Sister Iysra Thael

Positioned at the outer ring, close enough to observe, far enough that the substitution field is weakest. Not substituted — changed. Experiences the "verge effect": her identity feels slightly permeable without being threatening.

Her final message to Maeryth, the piece of truth Maeryth keeps from Selena: "The coherent ones chose their own anchors. That's what I've watched for years. The conditioning isn't what holds."

// THE_MIREVANE_ARC · IN_PRECISE_DETAIL //

The story's first death.
Earned architecturally, not explained.

Phase I
Initial contact (Serath): Approaches Mirevane already in records-crisis. Offers no answers — only questions. The conversation feels like being understood. First breach.
Phase II
Intellectual cultivation (Naeriva): Genuine scholarly interest across multiple encounters. Establishes: a records-keeper who can't verify her own records has a fundamental integrity problem. Not a threat — a diagnosis. Mirevane feels seen.
Phase III
The withdrawal: Naeriva does not recommend the green scan — Mirevane's instability would make results unreliable. Mirevane's professional identity cannot accept this. She asks for the scan herself.
Phase IV
What the scan does: Stabilizes her — genuinely, honestly. Now lucid for the first time since entering the colony, she requests voluntary substitution. The Sisters of Ytixus never suggested it. They only built the framework where the conclusion made sense.
Phase V
Soul-death: She was already an Altnet and never knew. The substitution hits a second threshold and collapses instantly. Her body remains for ~40 seconds while the soul-stack fails to re-stabilize. She is aware the entire time.

Her final expression is not rage. She is looking at her own records — not documents, the ones inside herself — and cannot determine which are hers. There was a self in there. She had been recording it all along. She just could not read her own handwriting.

Mirevane dies from an idea she chose. Kaelith dies from an action she chose. Mirevane's death says: the self cannot always think its way to safety. Kaelith's says: the self can sometimes act its way to meaning even when safety is impossible. Together they answer the story's central question — identity is not what you verify. It is what you do when verification isn't available.
