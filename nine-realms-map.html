// THE MYTH OF ELUNAR //

Nine stars fell from the void.
Where they struck, the Essence took root.

Before the nations, before the queens and gods, there was only the Celestial Aether — formless, unclaimed. When it fractured across the dark and fell to Elunar as nine burning stars, each impact seeded a different expression of that power into the land itself: fire into stone, frost into peak, shadow into hollow. The Nine Realms were not built. They were struck into being.
