// OPERATIVE PROFILE · THE SHADOW SEEKER //

A mind always at work.
A heart kept under deliberate lock.

Draven is a solitary figure by nature — preferring the dark, shadowed solitude of Drakenheim's mountain dungeons to the chaotic life of courtly intrigue. From a young age he was raised to endure extreme conditions, shaping him into a man of iron discipline and quiet resolve. This quiet demeanor conceals a mind always analyzing, calculating the smallest details, anticipating moves long before others conceive of them.

To those who know him, Draven is the epitome of self-control and caution. His training has hardened him not only physically but emotionally — he keeps his feelings buried beneath layers of discipline, lest they interfere with his duties. However, his visits to Sablea and his connection with Eris have started to erode some of this composure, hinting at a complexity few suspect. His feelings for her, though carefully concealed, remain one of his only weaknesses — an affection he is determined to bury for both their sakes.

"His loyalty to his duty, combined with the inherent risks of entangling himself in Vesper's web, forces him to maintain a strict boundary — even as he provides Eris with guidance and support."

Not a mount.
An extension of his will. A living embodiment of his darker nature.

Noctyss is as enigmatic as its rider. Their bond is symbiotic — sharing a psychic connection that transcends words, thoughts and intentions perfectly aligned in both battle and strategy. Together they are known as the Dreadwing. Their movements are perfectly synchronized in combat and reconnaissance. What Draven sees, Noctyss anticipates. What Noctyss burns, Draven already knew to direct.

// DRAGON_DESIGNATION: NOCTYSS //

Noctyss

The Soulflame Dragon · Dreadwing's Second Self

Shrouded in a dark, smoke-like aura and breathing purple flames that appear to consume both body and soul, Noctyss is a terrifying force on the battlefield. These unique Soulflame flames do not merely burn — they consume the essence of their victims, leaving behind only ash. The consumed souls become bound to the Eye of Ira pendant, deepening Draven's connection to dark magic with every strike.

Noctyss's presence amplifies Draven's command over the Umbral Essence — deepening his ability to manipulate shadows and dark magic beyond what he could achieve alone. The sight of Noctyss approaching has become, for the Vicious Tower Tribe, indistinguishable from a death sentence.

// BOND_TYPE: SYMBIOTIC · PSYCHIC · UNBREAKABLE //

The Bond

Psychic Synchronization · Neither Fully Dragon Nor Fully Human

Their bond allows them to perform feats of precision and power that surpass even the most skilled dragon-riders — their movements perfectly synchronized in combat and reconnaissance. They understand one another's intentions without a word. This unity is what makes them the Dreadwing: not one figure, but two entities functioning as a single force of calculated destruction.

In his Dragon Form, Noctyss and Draven become even more deeply merged. The Eye of Ira pendant draws on souls consumed by Noctyss's flames, creating a feedback loop of power that makes the combined entity something the Mountain Nation's records categorize simply as: singular threat, no standard countermeasure.

The Eye of Ira pendant is both Draven's greatest weapon and his curse. Crafted from the amethyst shards of Noctyss's egg and imbued with the souls consumed by its devastating purple flames, the pendant grants Draven control over dark magic far beyond the ordinary. The souls within empower him — enhancing physical strength, magical potency, and resilience with each addition to the reservoir.

He can summon Noctyss's Soulflame through the pendant — unique purple fire that does not merely burn, but consumes the essence of its victims, leaving behind only ash. These flames are feared across the realms: their effects are irreversible. The consumed souls become bound to the pendant, deepening his connection to his dragon and bolstering his magical reserves.

The pendant has become a symbol of fear among the Vicious Tower Tribe, who believe it grants Draven dominion over the souls it holds. This is not entirely inaccurate. Some tribespeople believe the pendant is the reason for their downfall — a cursed relic that saps their strength from beyond its wearer. They are not entirely wrong about that, either.

"The Vicious Tower Tribe's disdain for Draven has become legendary. He is more than just a scout to them — he is a mythic figure, an agent of death whose dark amethyst pendant is said to pulse with the souls of their fallen brethren."

The Dragon Form —
A Sinister Archangel

Draven's ability to transform into his Dragon Form is both a blessing and a curse — a manifestation of his hereditary power as a descendant of the Mountain Nation. In this form, he becomes an otherworldly figure of destruction, his human frame enhanced with draconic might and shadowy terror. No standard countermeasure exists.

// APPEARANCE: CLASSIFIED //

In Dragon Form, Draven's skin takes on a hardened, scale-like texture with an obsidian sheen. His eyes burn with violet fire. Purple flames seep from the corners of his mouth when power is fully unleashed. Ethereal shadow wings — shaped like jagged shards of night — sprout from his back, granting flight with unnerving grace. He is described by those who have survived encountering him as looking like a sinister archangel: death cloaked in shadow and flame.

// ABILITY_01 · SOULFLAME //
Soulflame Projection

He can breathe streams of purple fire devastating everything in their path. These flames consume magic and life alike, leaving nothing but scorched earth and ash. The souls of those consumed feed directly into the Eye of Ira pendant, sustaining the transformation and deepening his power simultaneously.

// ABILITY_02 · AMETHYST //
Amethyst Conduit

The Eye of Ira pendant glows fiercely during transformation, drawing in the energy of defeated foes to sustain the form. Allows Draven to absorb magic directed at him — converting it into raw power that can be unleashed as a destructive projectile or stored for later use. His enemies' strengths become his ammunition.

// ABILITY_03 · GOLDEN_BELT //
Golden Dragon Scale Belt

Crafted from Noctyss's own scales — serves as both armor and weapon. Absorbs and stores opposing magic, which can be redirected with devastating precision, doubling the original spell's destructive power. Fueled by the bond between Draven and Noctyss, it neutralizes powerful magical attacks and turns enemy strengths against them.

// WARNING · TRANSFORMATION_COST · CRITICAL //

While immensely powerful, Draven's Dragon Form comes with significant risks. The transformation consumes the souls stored within the Eye of Ira at an alarming rate. If the reservoir runs dry, the form begins to siphon his own life force, weakening him with each passing moment. To sustain the form, Draven must remain in constant motion — attacking relentlessly to feed the pendant and prevent it from turning on him. This creates a fighting style that cannot pause, cannot defend, cannot afford mercy. The form that makes him nearly unstoppable also makes him incapable of stopping.

// SABLEAN_ALLIANCE //
// EXTERNAL_OPERATIONS · SABLEA · CLASSIFIED_ALLIANCE //

He arrives unannounced.
He is always expected.

Draven's dealings with the Sablean Sisters are shrouded in secrecy. He visits Sablea under the cover of night, arriving unannounced and slipping into the Ebony Towers through discreet pathways known only to a select few. His loyalty to the Mountain Nation demands independence — but the mutual respect between him and both queens allows for a pragmatic alliance that benefits all three.

🩸
Queen Vesper
// TACTICAL_ASSET · POLITICAL_INTELLIGENCE //

Vesper sees Draven as a useful asset — particularly his ability to move undetected and gather secrets from other nations. She grants him safe passage through Sablea and shares secret intelligence, entrusting him with information that could destabilize enemy territories. In return, Draven reports pertinent knowledge from his scouting missions — particularly intel on the Vicious Tower Tribe, whose ambition threatens Vesper's regional control. His reports allow the Sisters to counteract threats before they reach Sablea's borders.

🌒
Queen Vashti
// ARCANE_KINSHIP · UMBRAL_ESSENCE //

Vashti values Draven for his expertise in dark magic and his connection to the Umbral Essence. She sees in him a kindred spirit — their exchanges often silent, steeped in unspoken understanding. Both possess an affinity for darkness and secrecy that fosters a unique bond. She occasionally requests his assistance with arcane rituals requiring his power and presence. He frequently transports magical artifacts or cryptic messages to and from the Council of Shadows across realms, acting as an intermediary for forbidden knowledge.

🏜
Sandstrider Mercenary Guild
// OPERATIONAL_COORDINATION · MUTUAL_RESPECT //

The SMG coordinates with Draven on missions involving reconnaissance and covert strikes against the Vicious Tower Tribe. Their alliance is one of mutual respect — the mercenaries acknowledge his superior scouting and tactical skills, while Draven values their agility and knowledge of desert terrain. He scouts the mountain ranges identifying weaknesses in the Tribe's fortresses; they provide the desert intelligence he cannot gather alone. His methods are swift and ruthless — often diminishing the Tribe's forces before they realize he's been there.

// ADVERSARY_FILE · THE VICIOUS TOWER TRIBE · DREADWING'S BANE //

The Vicious Tower Tribe's disdain for Draven has become legendary. Known as Dreadwing among their warriors, he is viewed as the embodiment of terror — an avenging shadow with a dragon's soulflame at his command. The tribe's attempts to rival Sablea often end in ruin, foiled by his reconnaissance or brutal ambushes. The legend of Dreadwing has grown so potent that some tribespeople believe his pendant is the reason for their downfall — a cursed relic that saps their strength. When Draven, accompanied by the SMG, descends upon their mountain camps, his precision is merciless. He strikes without warning, often weakening them to the point that the SMG can finish the job without substantial resistance. Though the tribe despises him, they have come to respect his skill — the younger warriors whispering of Dreadwing as both a nightmare and a challenge to be overcome. He is, to them, mythology made real.

Eris

The Hidden Heir · Queen Vesper's Secret Daughter · His Only Acknowledged Weakness

In the shadows of the Sablean Exchange or the moonlit corridors of the Ebony Towers, they share quiet moments — her questions and curiosity drawing him out of his guarded shell in ways few others can. Eris sees Draven as a mentor, a mysterious figure who has walked the path of dark magic she dreams of mastering. She confides in him, seeking his wisdom on everything from the nuances of magic to the complexities of navigating the Sablean court.

Draven, in turn, finds himself drawn to her innocence and ambition. He senses her untapped potential and recognizes that her fate is tangled with the Sablean Sisters' schemes in ways she does not yet understand. Despite his feelings for her, he understands that any romantic pursuit would place both of them in grave danger. His loyalty to his duty, combined with the inherent risks of entangling himself in Vesper's web, forces him to maintain a strict boundary.

He gives her fragments of advice that may aid her in the future — subtly steering her away from the more destructive paths that Vashti and Vesper have laid before her. His affection remains concealed beneath a veneer of formality. He dreams of a life beyond the shadows. He knows he must tread carefully. These two facts exist in him simultaneously, and have for some time, and he has not yet found a resolution between them. He is not sure one exists.
