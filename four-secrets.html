Origins · Born of Defiance Against Natural Law

Their conception defied
the gods themselves.

The bloodborne children are more than heirs — they are symbols of defiance against natural law and the gods themselves. Conceived through a blend of blood magic and lunar alignment, their births were marked by foreboding omens: eclipses, blood-red rains, and the howling of unseen beasts in the desert.

"Their father, King Dante II of Ashura, remains an enigmatic figure in their story. His absence from their lives has only deepened the brothers' sense of betrayal and fueled their hatred for the kingdom of Ashura."

— The Winding Veins Archive · Restricted Access · Council of Shadows
Method of CreationBlood magic and celestial alchemy — miscarried pregnancies resurrected through forbidden ritual. Not human infants. Powerful beings bound to Vesper's will.
Birth OmensEclipses. Blood-red rains. The howling of unseen beasts in the desert. Each birth marked by foreboding visible across Sablea's night sky.
Their Father's RoleKing Dante II of Ashura. Whether a willing participant in the dark ritual remains unclear. His abandonment of Vesper fueled shared hatred for Ashura's kingdom.
What They AreLiving manifestations of blood magic, shadow, and lunar power. Each embodies a facet of Sablea's dark legacy. Raised in secrecy beneath the Winding Veins.
Their BaseThe Winding Veins — the labyrinthine network beneath Sablea. Hidden from almost everyone in the Nine Realms. Their existence is one of the most closely guarded secrets.
The ParadoxAs much as they serve their mother, they harbor a shared desire to carve their own legacy — untethered from the shadows of their lineage. Tools that have begun to sharpen themselves.

I
The Eldest · First SecretKeeper of the Blood Oath
Role: Enforcer · Unity · Fear

Loryxan

The Cornerstone · The Unbreakable Contract

The eldest and most enigmatic — the cornerstone of the siblings' unity and Vesper's greatest weapon. Calm and calculating, he enforces the blood oaths that bind Sablea's agents and allies, ensuring loyalty through fear and pain. His presence is an imposing blend of authority and quiet menace, his voice carrying the weight of unbreakable contracts.

Beyond Sablea, Loryxan operates independently as a shadow broker across all Nine Realms — owning the loopholes in Dante I's Sablean accords and running the Crimson Covenant playbook without direct oversight. He may have outgrown his forge.

[ Hover to reveal abilities ]
Oathbinding Ritual

Through intricate blood magic, enforces oaths with devastating consequences for betrayal. Those who break their word are cursed — suffering excruciating agony or death.

Bloodborne Ward

Shields his siblings with powerful wards, bolstering their defenses against physical and magical attacks simultaneously. The only one who can protect all four at once.

Vyrnax's Blessing

A gift from Vashti's secret rituals — can shapeshift into shadowed forms, adapting to any threat or situation. The most versatile ability held by any of the four.

What Drives Him

Loyalty to Vesper and the desire to preserve his family's unity. But his role as enforcer weighs heavily. He wonders whether their collective secrecy will be their undoing. He is the one most likely to break the cycle — or seal it permanently.

II
The Third-Born · Third SecretThe Blade of Midnight
Role: Diplomat · Warrior · Ether-Traveler

Thalrik

The Disciplined · The Balance Between Light and Darkness

A disciplined and honorable warrior who embodies the balance between light and darkness. He values personal redemption — seeking to define his identity beyond his mother's shadow. He is the diplomat among the brothers, navigating the delicate politics of the Nine Realms with both charm and menace.

Thalrik holds the most significant power of the four: mastery of the Ether-realm allows him to traverse the Abyssal Gate — the only being who can bring his brothers through to New Sablea without succumbing to its deadly temporal effects. The key to the future.

[ Hover to reveal abilities ]
Midnight Resonance

Under moonlight, physical abilities are enhanced — making him a nearly unstoppable combatant. His full power is available only in darkness.

Lunar Slash

Channels the moon's energy into a blade capable of cutting through enchanted steel. The blow leaves wounds that do not respond to conventional healing.

Eternal Duelist

Can isolate himself and an enemy in an ethereal arena — an uninterrupted duel where no outside interference is possible. The fight ends when he decides it does.

What Drives Him

Balance between serving his family and pursuing his own sense of honor. He often questions the morality of their actions but remains loyal — hoping to one day redeem their legacy. Of the four, he is most likely to choose differently when the moment truly demands it.

III
The Second-Born · Second SecretThe Scourge of Shadows
Role: Aggression · Expansion · Terror

Calivor

The Rage · The Desperate Hunger for Validation

The embodiment of rage and despair — channeling his destructive impulses into devastating campaigns that expand Sablea's influence. Aggressive and confrontational, often clashing with his brothers over strategy. Yet beneath his fury lies a deep-seated insecurity and a desperate need for validation.

Of all the brothers, Calivor represents the most direct threat to Vesper's throne. His political ambitions have grown too visible for comfort. He studies her methods, catalogs her alliances, and waits. His rage is a weapon. His patience is the danger.

[ Hover to reveal abilities ]
Eclipse Cascade

Summons waves of shadow energy — extinguishing all light and trapping enemies in suffocating darkness. He controls everything within his shadow.

Soul Shackle

Can bind an enemy's shadow — rendering them immobile or forcing them to act against their will. The most terrifying control ability in the brothers' collective arsenal.

Oblivion Edge

His blade, forged from shadow essence, grows stronger with every soul it claims. The longer a battle runs, the more dangerous he becomes.

What Drives Him

A relentless pursuit of power fueled by the desire to prove his worth to Vesper, to the gods, to anyone willing to acknowledge him. The most unpredictable of the four. The most likely to break everything before he gets what he wants.

IV
The Youngest · Fourth SecretThe Blood Reaper
Role: Manipulation · Subversion · Blood Sorcery

Sanguinor

The Coldest · The Pain Beneath the Cruelty

The youngest and most sinister — a master manipulator who uses blood magic to sow fear and chaos. Cold and calculating, he thrives on the suffering of his enemies, using their life essence to fuel his dark sorcery. Despite his cruel demeanor, he harbors a deep void of pain caused by his unacknowledged lineage.

Of the four, Sanguinor is the most emotionally volatile — because his coldness is a performance. Beneath it is a yearning for the acceptance and love that he has been denied since before he was born. The cruelest actions come from the deepest wounds.

[ Hover to reveal abilities ]
Blood Dominion

Can manipulate the blood of his enemies — creating weapons, shields, or waves of crimson energy. Battlefields favor him exponentially. The more blood, the greater his power.

Revenant's Veil

Becomes incorporeal — cloaked in a mist of blood and shadow, physically untouchable for a short time. Used to reposition, escape, or pass through defenses.

Crimson Rebirth

Through blood rituals, can resurrect himself or allies — at great personal cost. He has used it. He knows the price. He would use it again.

What Drives Him

Recognition and revenge. His cold demeanor masks a yearning for the acceptance and love he has been denied. His cruelty is inversely proportional to the size of the wound beneath it. The day someone truly acknowledges him may be the most dangerous day in the Nine Realms.

Their Function Within Vesper's Shadow Empire

The shadows that
shape the Nine Realms.

Together they form a fearsome unit capable of dismantling armies, toppling governments, and spreading Vesper's influence far beyond Sablea's borders. Each leads his own faction within the Winding Veins.

🩸Loryxan
Political Influence

Uses his mastery of blood oaths to bind foreign leaders and allies to Vesper's cause — ensuring their loyalty through the ever-present fear of betrayal. His network spans the Nine Realms independently.

⚔Thalrik
Military Power & Covert Operations

Leads covert operations that weaken Sablea's enemies — often engaging in diplomatic subterfuge to avoid open conflict. His Ether-realm mastery allows him to appear and disappear from any battlefield without warning.

🌑Calivor
Territorial Expansion

Spearheads campaigns that extend Sablea's reach into neighboring realms — using his shadow magic to instill terror and submission. He does not negotiate territory. He takes it.

👁Sanguinor
Espionage & Subversion

His manipulative tactics sow discord among enemy factions — turning allies against one another. Wars he starts look like accidents. Nations he destabilizes believe they did it themselves.
